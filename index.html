From juice to glass

The five stages of every batch

The details change with the fruit, but the shape of the journey is always the same.

  1. Prepare

    Sanitise everything, prepare the must and take a starting gravity.

  2. Ferment

    Pitch the yeast and let it turn sugar into alcohol over one to two weeks.

  3. Rack

    Move the wine off its sediment into a clean vessel under an airlock.

  4. Clear and age

    Stabilise, let it fall bright, and give it time to round out.

  5. Bottle

    Bottle when it is stable and clear, then label it and wait a little longer.
